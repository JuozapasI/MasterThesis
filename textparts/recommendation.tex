The unassigned reads are present in the scRNA-seq datasets, and some of them contain biologically meaningful information.
Hence it is recommended not to discard those unassigned reads completely.
Particularly, those that are located in isolated regions
(not on the oposite strand of known genes) might be unannotated genes.
This should especially be important in the case when new or less explored organizms are studied,
as their transcriptomic annotation might be not comprehensive.

Also, resolving overlaps in the genome annotation might help to have more genes in the downstream analysis.
Therefore, if one wants to have as much information as possible about the sample of the study,
one should consider using some tool to deal with multimappers.
