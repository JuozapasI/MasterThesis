\begin{itemize}
  \item The single cell RNA sequencing data contains reads that are mapped to the genome, but not assigned to any gene.
  The amount of such reads varied between 11-29\% in datasets analyzed.
  
  \item The unassigned reads mapped to the antisense strands of known genes are likely artifacts produces during reverse
  transcription reaction.
  It should be noted that this applies only to short-read 3' end sequencing protocols.
  
  \item The unassigned reads mapped to locations without known genes present on any strand might be originating from
  unannotated genes.
  This thesis suggested a list of 147 such candidates.
  The type of the experiments (short-read 3' end sequencing) has not allowed to infer global structures of those pottentionally novel genes.
  
  \item Some of the unassigned reads that were mapped to the locations containing overlapping genes mights be included
  into downstream analysis by resolving gene overlaps in the transcriptomic annotation.
  The proposed approach allowed to have ???-???\% more reads in the cell-gene matrices used in the downstream analysis.
\end{itemize}
